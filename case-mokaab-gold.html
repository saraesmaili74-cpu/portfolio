CASE STUDY / 05 · 2025

Mokaab
Gold

A clearer way to buy, hold, and understand physical and digital gold and silver.

Digital precious-metals platformEnd-to-end Product · UI/UX DesignProject commission

01 / OVERVIEW

One experience for two forms of value.

Mokaab Gold brings digital and physical gold and silver into one online experience. I owned the product design from the first structure and screen flows through responsive UI, design system, and the public landing page.

My role
Product Designer
UI/UX Designer
Ownership

Engagement
Project-based commission
End to end
Scope
Digital gold, physical metals, pricing, wallet, checkout, delivery, and account flows

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Make a complex purchase feel dependable.

The product combines live prices, different asset types, payment choices, delivery, and account verification. The experience needed to make those decisions legible on desktop and mobile without hiding important context.

03 / UX & RESEARCH PROCESS

From evidence to a connected product

I reviewed the market, translated user stories into screen flows, mapped the information structure, then used responsive wireframes and a shared component system to move into high-fidelity UI.

01 / COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

Learn the category before shaping it

I compared local and international products to understand trust signals, onboarding, pricing, product discovery, and the hand-off between digital ownership and physical delivery.

02 / STRUCTURE

Organise the decisions users make

User stories clarified the jobs behind registration, buying, selling, tracking price, and receiving physical assets. The site map then connected those jobs into a navigable product structure.

03 / SCREEN FLOWS

Map the path before styling it

Instead of a single linear user flow, I used screen flows with wireframes to show the states and decisions in each scenario: authentication, market price, physical delivery, and dashboard use.

04 / DESIGN SYSTEM

Build once, stay consistent

I defined the foundations and reusable components before completing the UI: colour, type, spacing, cards, inputs, pricing states, navigation, and feedback patterns.

05 / RESPONSIVE UI

The same product at every size

High-fidelity screens carry the same hierarchy from desktop to tablet and mobile. Related views are presented together so the responsive decisions remain visible.

06 / FINAL UI

A product people can read at a glance

The final interface brings together product discovery, price context, wallet actions, and trust cues in a calm visual system designed for confident decisions.

07 / REFLECTION

Design the decision, not just the screen.

The strongest outcome was a connected system: flows made the complex scenarios explicit, the design system kept the UI consistent, and responsive layouts let the same product logic work across devices.
