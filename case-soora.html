CASE STUDY / 06 · 2025–PRESENT

Soora

An AI-assisted astrology app and web app that turns a birth chart into personal, relationship, and daily readings.

Independent product conceptProduct · UI/UX · Prompt designMobile app & web appGet it on Bazaar ↗Get it on Myket ↗

01 / OVERVIEW

Astrology data, made personal and easier to explore.

Soora is a product concept for people who want to understand their birth chart, everyday planetary transits, and relationship compatibility through a guided experience. The product uses structured astrology data and carefully written AI prompts to shape each reading.

02 / OPPORTUNITY

Turn complex chart data into a path people can follow.

A birth chart can feel dense and unfamiliar. The product needed a clear entry point, understandable report types, and a respectful way to guide users from their own chart to daily insights and relationship compatibility.

03 / PRODUCT PROCESS

From idea to an explorable MVP

I framed the concept, mapped the main journeys, tested the early MVP before build, then designed the interface and prompt patterns that connect chart data to readable reports.

01 / CORE FLOWS

Start with the information needed for a personal reading.

The first flow gathers the details needed to create a birth chart, then gives users a clear route into the report that matters to them. This keeps the first experience focused rather than presenting every interpretation at once.

02 / PROMPT DESIGN

Give AI a clear role in the reading.

I wrote prompts to turn structured astrology inputs into focused report formats: personality and life, relationships and marriage, career and life direction, and compatibility between two charts. Prompt patterns define the context, output structure, and tone so each report stays scannable and relevant to its scenario.

Personal chart

Personality, life themes, and career direction from one chart.

Compatibility

Emotional, friendship, and work dynamics between two charts.

Daily transit

A daily reading based on current planetary transits and the user’s chart.

03 / READING EXPERIENCES

Separate questions, clear report formats.

The experience distinguishes between an ongoing daily reading and a deeper compatibility report. Each report has its own entry point and hierarchy instead of treating every astrology question as the same screen.

04 / REFLECTION

Designing an AI experience means designing the question too.

Soora brought product flow, interface design, and prompt writing into one system. The work focused on helping users understand what they can ask, what information is needed, and how to move from raw chart data to a meaningful report.
